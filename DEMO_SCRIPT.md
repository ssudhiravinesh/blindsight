# BLINDSIGHT — Video Demo Script

> **Target Duration:** ~3 minutes  
> **Format:** Screen recording + voiceover  
> **Tip:** Keep a calm, confident pace. Let the visuals breathe.

---

## SCENE 1 — INTRO (0:00 – 0:25)

**Screen:** BLINDSIGHT logo / extension icon — or a clean title card.

**Narration:**
> *"Every day, billions of people click 'I Agree' without reading a single line of what they're consenting to. 97% of users never read Terms of Service — and buried in those agreements are clauses that sell your data, strip your legal rights, and sign away your content forever."*
>
> *"BLINDSIGHT changes that. It's an AI-powered Chrome extension that acts as a legal firewall — intercepting you at the exact moment of consent, analysing the terms in real-time, and stopping you before you sign away something you shouldn't."*
>
> *"Let me show you how it works."*

---

## SCENE 2 — SIGN-UP DETECTION (0:25 – 0:55)

**Screen:** Open Chrome → navigate to `accounts.google.com/signup`

**Action:** As the page loads, BLINDSIGHT's detection banner slides in from the top.

**Narration:**
> *"The moment I land on a sign-up page, BLINDSIGHT's detection engine kicks in. Under the hood, it runs a heuristic confidence-scoring system — it looks at the page's DOM for password fields, sign-up buttons, terms checkboxes, and URL patterns. Each signal has a weight, and when the combined score crosses the threshold, it fires."*
>
> *"Right now, it's detected this Google sign-up page and is asking me: 'Would you like to scan the Terms of Service before you continue?'"*
>
> *"Let's click Scan Terms."*

**Action:** Click **"Scan Terms"** → scanning animation plays.

---

## SCENE 3 — TIER 1: GREEN / STANDARD (0:55 – 1:20)

**Screen:** Navigate to **wikipedia.org** (or **duckduckgo.com**) sign-up/ToS page → trigger scan.

**Action:** Scan completes → **Green / Grade A** toast notification: *"Terms Look Good."*

**Narration:**
> *"Let's start with a service that gets it right. Wikipedia — an open, non-profit platform. BLINDSIGHT scans their Terms of Service and classifies it as **Tier 0, Standard** — a green grade A. The terms are clean. No data selling, no content grabs, no tricks. Just a simple, non-intrusive toast confirming that you're safe to proceed."*

---

## SCENE 4 — TIER 2: YELLOW / NOTABLE (1:20 – 1:50)

**Screen:** Navigate to a **Google / Amazon / Microsoft** sign-up page → trigger scan.

**Action:** Scan completes → **Yellow / Grade B** toast with clause summary.

**Narration:**
> *"Now let's try Google. This time, BLINDSIGHT returns a **yellow, Grade B — Notable**. This means the terms are mostly standard, but there are a few clauses worth knowing about. You'll see a summary right here — things like broad content licensing or data usage for service improvement. Nothing predatory, but not invisible either. BLINDSIGHT lets you proceed, but now you know what you're agreeing to."*

---

## SCENE 5 — TIER 3: ORANGE / CAUTIONARY (1:50 – 2:20)

**Screen:** Navigate to **robinhood.com** sign-up page → trigger scan.

**Action:** Scan completes → **Orange / Grade C** full-screen overlay with clause list. Accept button is **blocked with a 5-second countdown timer**.

**Narration:**
> *"Here's where things get serious. Robinhood — and BLINDSIGHT returns **orange, Grade C — Cautionary**. Now watch what happens: a full-screen overlay appears listing every concerning clause. And the Accept button? It's locked behind a 5-second countdown timer. You physically cannot agree until the timer runs out. This is BLINDSIGHT's graduated intervention — the risk is real, so it slows you down and makes you read."*
>
> *"It also suggests privacy-respecting alternatives you can use instead."*

---

## SCENE 6 — TIER 4: RED / CRITICAL (2:20 – 2:55)

**Screen:** Navigate to **temu.com** (or **shein.in** / **clearview.ai**) → trigger scan.

**Action:** Scan completes → **Red / Grade F** full-screen overlay. Accept button is **locked until the user types "I PROCEED"**.

**Narration:**
> *"And now — the red zone. Temu. BLINDSIGHT returns **Grade F — Critical**. Predatory data-selling clauses, broad content licensing, class-action waivers — the works. The overlay is up. The clause list is damning. And this time, the Accept button isn't on a timer — it's completely locked. The only way past this is to type the exact phrase: 'I PROCEED'."*
>
> *"This isn't just a warning. It's a checkpoint. You cannot claim you didn't know."*

**Action:** Type **"I PROCEED"** → button unlocks → but don't click it. Dismiss instead.

---

## SCENE 7 — ENDING (2:55 – 3:15)

**Screen:** Return to the extension popup — show scan history with all 4 tiers visible. Optionally show the alternatives database.

**Narration:**
> *"BLINDSIGHT doesn't just protect you once — it remembers. Every scan is logged. And in the background, it monitors your tracked services for ToS changes, re-analysing and alerting you if the terms get worse."*
>
> *"97% of people never read the Terms of Service. BLINDSIGHT reads them for you — and makes sure 'I Agree' actually means something."*
>
> *"BLINDSIGHT. Your legal firewall for the internet."*

**Screen:** End card — project name, team name, logo.

---

## Recording Tips

- **Resolution:** 1920×1080, browser at ~90% zoom for clean visuals.
- **Browser:** Use a clean Chrome profile with no other extensions visible.
- **Tab management:** Pre-open each site in separate tabs so you can switch quickly.
- **Pace:** Don't rush the scans — let the scanning animation play fully.
- **Cursor:** Move the cursor deliberately. No frantic mouse movements.
- **Audio:** Record voiceover separately if possible for cleaner audio; overlay in post.
